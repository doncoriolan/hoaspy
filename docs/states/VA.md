## Where members look things up

From `site/portals.json` (hand-maintained; drives the Find & add records page).

**Courts**

| Portal | Scope | Access | What to type | Indexed by HOA Spy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Virginia circuit courts — case information](https://eapps.courts.state.va.us/CJISWeb/circuit.jsp) | statewide (most circuit courts) | free | Pick the court, then name search. Fairfax and Alexandria keep their own systems. |  |
| [Virginia general district courts — case information](https://eapps.courts.state.va.us/gdcourts/) | statewide | captcha | Small-claims and civil under $25k; a captcha precedes the session. |  |

**Liens / recorded documents**

- [Circuit court clerk — land records](https://www.vacourts.gov/courts/circuit/) — Liens are recorded with the circuit court clerk; the 'Secure Remote Access' index is subscription in most counties.

**Corporate record**

- [Virginia SCC — Clerk's Information System](https://cis.scc.virginia.gov/)

**HOA registry**

- [Virginia Common Interest Community Board — association registration](https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/) — Registered associations and complaint process.

## Not freely accessible

- circuit-court land records — paid per-clerk Secure Remote Access

## Collect and rebuild

```bash
./venv/bin/python -m hoaspy.collect.registries.get_states --state VA
./venv/bin/python -m hoaspy.collect.registries.get_irs_bmf --state VA
./venv/bin/python -m hoaspy.collect.courts.get_courts            # nationwide; checkpointed
./venv/bin/python -m hoaspy.collect.courts.get_state_courts --portal va_gdc
./venv/bin/python -m hoaspy.build.build_site
./venv/bin/python -m hoaspy.build.build_map
./venv/bin/python -m hoaspy.build.build_seo --base-url https://hoaspy.com
./venv/bin/python -m hoaspy.build.make_states_md   # refresh this page
```

Collectors take `--no-upload` where they can push to S3; the mirror is pushed explicitly with `./scripts/s3_push_all.sh` ([S3.md](../S3.md)). How to add a source: PIPELINE.md.

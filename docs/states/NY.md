**Courts**

| Portal | Scope | Access | What to type | Indexed by HOA Spy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [NYSCEF — e-filed case search](https://iapps.courts.state.ny.us/nyscef/CaseSearch) | statewide (e-filed Supreme Court cases) | free | Party name search; every e-filed Supreme Court case with its documents. |  |

**Liens / recorded documents**

- [NYC ACRIS](https://a836-acris.nyc.gov/DS/DocumentSearch/PartyName) — New York City — Party-name search of every recorded document in the five boroughs; HOA Spy indexes condo liens from it.
- [County clerk — land records (outside NYC)](https://www.nysac.org/) — Liens are recorded with the county clerk.

## Where members look things up

**Liens / recorded documents**

- [County clerk — real property records](https://www.txdirectory.com/online/county/) — Assessment liens and notices of foreclosure sale are filed with the county clerk; most large counties have a free online index (e.g. Harris, Dallas, Travis, Bexar).

**Corporate record**

- [Texas Secretary of State — SOSDirect](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/corp/sosda/index.shtml)

**HOA registry**

- [TREC — HOA management certificates](https://hoa.texas.gov/management-certificates-search) — Every Texas HOA must file a management certificate; HOA Spy holds these.

## Not freely accessible

- TX Secretary of State corporate bulk data — paid (SOSDirect)
- county recorder lien indexes — no free bulk source found (county-by-county paywalls)
